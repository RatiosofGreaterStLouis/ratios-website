WHAT IT IS

Built to help families

emergency.

OneProfile™ gives families one organized place to keep the details that matter most: medical notes, emergency contacts, communication preferences, sensory needs, comfort strategies, and safety instructions.

HOW IT WORKS

From caregiver information to a connected safety tool.

1

Create the profile

Add key details about your loved one, including photo, preferred name, emergency contacts, medical notes, and communication style.

2

Keep details current

Update contacts, medications, caregiver notes, triggers, calming strategies, and other important information.

3

Connect an identifier

Issued RATIOS QR codes, safety cards, or LifePatch™ products link to the profile's emergency information.

4

Scan when needed

First responders and trusted caregivers can quickly access the information the family has chosen to share.

COMMON QUESTIONS

OneProfile™ FAQ

Ask a Question →
Is OneProfile™ available now?

Yes. Enrollment and secure caregiver access are available now. Connected QR safety identifiers are issued and activated through the RATIOS program.

Will families control what is shared?

Caregivers manage the information in the profile and the emergency information associated with their participant's issued identifier.

How do QR safety products connect?

Each issued QR identifier uses its own scan link to open the emergency information associated with that identifier.
