What it is

Built to help families prepare before an emergency.

OneProfile™ keeps important details together, including medical notes, emergency contacts, communication preferences, sensory needs, comfort strategies, safety risks, and emergency instructions.

Enrollment and secure caregiver access are available now. Connected profile tools are managed through the OneProfile™ system as they are issued and activated.

How it works

From caregiver information to a connected safety tool.

01

Create the profile

Add the information responders or trusted caregivers may need to understand the individual and provide appropriate support.

02

Keep details current

Update contacts, medications, communication needs, triggers, calming supports, and other important safety information.

03

Connect an identifier

Issued QR and compatible NFC safety products connect the identifier to its OneProfile™ emergency information.

04

Scan when needed

Scanning the connected identifier opens the emergency view associated with that code so relevant information can be accessed quickly.

What can be stored

Person-centered details in one place.

The profile is designed around practical information families may need to communicate repeatedly with caregivers, schools, programs, healthcare teams, and first responders.

Connected safety tools

One profile. Multiple ways to help make it accessible.

OneProfile™ is the information layer behind RATIOS safety identifiers. The product carries the connection; the profile carries the information.

QR

QR Safety Card

A scannable option for a wallet, backpack, binder, emergency folder, or other accessible location.

NFC

NFC Tag

A tap-to-open connection for compatible smartphones and supported RATIOS safety products.

LP

LifePatch™

A wearable safety identifier designed to connect the wearer with their associated emergency information.

ID

Emergency Information

The connected view is tied to the issued identifier rather than a generic or simulated public profile.

What happens after a scan

The QR code opens the information connected to that identifier.

There is no fake sample profile shown here. An issued RATIOS identifier uses its own scan link to open the emergency information connected to that code. What is available depends on the profile and identifier configuration.

Common questions

OneProfile™ FAQ

Is OneProfile™ available now?

Enrollment and secure caregiver access are available now. Connected profile and identifier functions are managed within the OneProfile™ system as they are issued and activated.

Can families update profile information?

Yes. OneProfile™ is designed so caregiver-managed information can be kept current as contacts, medications, needs, or safety information change.

How do QR and NFC products connect?

Issued identifiers are associated with the appropriate OneProfile™ record. Scanning or tapping a connected identifier opens the emergency information associated with that identifier.
