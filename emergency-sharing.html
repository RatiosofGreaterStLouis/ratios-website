01 / RECOGNIZED & CONNECTED

Basic emergency card

When enabled, this card stays visible even when full emergency profile sharing is off.

  • Participant’s name and preferred name
  • Primary emergency contact: name, relationship, and phone
  • Alternate emergency contact: name and phone, when provided

Names and contacts come from your saved participant profile. Add or update them there. The current profile stores the participant’s first name.

This short note is visible on the basic card. Include only information you want anyone scanning the identifier to see.

02 / ADDITIONAL SUPPORT

Full emergency profile

Select the additional information someone scanning may see. Basic card names and contacts are included automatically when the basic card is enabled. Medical history, medications, allergies, destinations, and other details appear only when selected here and full sharing is on.

Need to pause everything? Turn off both the basic emergency card and full profile sharing, then save. Inactive or replaced identifiers remain unavailable regardless of these settings.
