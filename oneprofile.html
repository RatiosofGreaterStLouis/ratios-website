What’s included with your free OneProfile™ membership

Your membership includes access to OneProfile™, your first personalized ID card, and a custom emergency fridge magnet. Both items have a personal QR code that links to your loved one’s OneProfile emergency profile. No shop purchase is required.

First card included free

A personalized ID card to carry

Your first ID card is personalized for the individual, with their own QR code linked to their OneProfile emergency profile. Keep it with them so support information and emergency contacts are easy to find when needed.

Custom magnet included free

A quick reference for caregivers at home

Your custom fridge magnet includes the individual’s personal QR code. Place it somewhere easy to see so a relative, babysitter, respite worker, or other caregiver can quickly find emergency information while the primary caregiver is away.

They can scan the QR code to view the emergency profile without needing to manage the OneProfile account. The primary caregiver continues to manage and update the profile.
