OneProfile™ gives families one organized place to keep the details that matter most: medical notes, emergency contacts, communication preferences, sensory needs, comfort strategies, and safety instructions.

ⓘEnrollment and secure caregiver access are now available.
Additional emergency-profile tools are being developed in protected phases.

●AccessibleDesigned for families, caregivers, schools, programs, and first responders.

♥Purpose-drivenCreated to support safety, dignity, and better outcomes.

From caregiver information to a connected safety tool.

1

Create the profile

Add key details about your loved one, including photo, preferred name, emergency contacts, medical notes, and communication style.

2

Keep details current

Update contacts, medications, caregiver notes, triggers, calming strategies, and other important information.

3

Connect an identifier

Issued QR codes, safety cards, or LifePatch™ products link to the profile.

4

Scan when needed

First responders and trusted caregivers can quickly access the information you choose to share.
