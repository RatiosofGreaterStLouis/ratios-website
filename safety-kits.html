Inside the Kit

Essential Tools for Real-Life Situations.

Each Safety Kit includes practical resources to help caregivers prepare, respond, and keep their loved ones safe.

Emergency IDQuick access to important identifying and caregiver information.
Caregiver FormKeep essential contacts and communication details together.
Current PhotoPhoto and physical-description planning for a missing-person emergency.
Action ChecklistSimple preparation steps families can organize before a crisis.
ID

Emergency identification card

Quick access to critical information when it matters most.

CC

Caregiver contact & communication form

Keep important contacts and details in one place.

PH

Photo & physical description template

Helpful in the event a loved one goes missing.

MS

Medical, behavioral & sensory needs

Organize key health and support information.

AW

Awareness resources

Support recognition, visibility, and understanding.

✓

Emergency action checklist

Simple steps for greater preparation and confidence.

Why It Matters

More Than a Kit — It’s Peace of Mind.

Our Safety Kits provide practical support that can make a critical difference during an emergency. They help first responders, caregivers, and community members quickly access important information, reduce stress, and improve outcomes for individuals and families.
